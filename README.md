## Sync Pulse

### Fitness tracking, redesigned around understanding.

Sync Pulse is a fitness tracking experience focused on making health information easier to interpret and everyday progress easier to understand.

Instead of treating the product as a collection of dashboards, the design explores how **hierarchy, context, and visual feedback** can make tracking feel more approachable.

<br>

<table>
<tr>
<td width="50%" valign="top">

**THE PROBLEM**

Fitness applications can expose users to a large amount of information without making the most important insight obvious.

</td>

<td width="50%" valign="top">

**THE APPROACH**

Create a clearer experience where users can quickly understand their current state, progress, and next action.

</td>
</tr>
</table>
